Learning Seminar on Kazhdan-Lusztig theory

POSTECH Fall 2026

General Information: This is the webpage for the POSTECH Learning Seminar in representation theory from Fall 2026. The topic is Kazhdan-Lusztig theory.

Organizers: Valentin Buciumas, buciumas at postech dot ac dot kr.

Time and Place: Mondays 3:30pm-5:30pm in Room 404 (math building) unless otherwise noted.

Topic: Kazhdan-Lusztig theory. More precisely Hecke algebras, Kazhdan-Lusztig basis and polynomials, as well as generalizations and connections to (mostly) representation theory, geometry and combinatorics.
